# Lists

A tight list:

- one
- two
- three

A loose list:

- one

- two

- three

An ordered list starting at seven:

7. seven
8. eight

An ordered list that does not increment:

1. one
1. two
1. three

A list item with two paragraphs:

- first paragraph

  second paragraph

- another item

A list item that starts with a fence:

- ```js
  const a = 1;
  ```

A list item that starts with a nested list:

-
  - inner first

A list with a blank item:

- one
-
- three
